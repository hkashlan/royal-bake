Meet chef Abu-Abdo!

With over three decades of culinary expertise, Abu-Abdo is a seasoned master in the art of baking and gourmet food preparation. Known for his passion, precision, and creativity in the kitchen, Abu-Abdo has built a distinguished career blending traditional techniques with innovative flair. From artisan breads and delicate pastries to full-scale menu development, their work reflects a deep commitment to quality, flavor, and culinary excellence.

Throughout a career spanning more than 30 years, Abu-Abdo has led more than 3 restaurants, bakery kitchens, trained aspiring chefs, and curated unforgettable dining experiences. His extensive knowledge of ingredients, textures, and flavor profiles makes him not just a chef—but a true craftsman dedicated to the culinary arts. Whether behind the scenes or in front of an audience, Abu-Abdo brings heart, heritage, and a touch of perfection to every dish.
